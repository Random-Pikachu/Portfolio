# 03 · Firmament (6000×1500)

A wide, unbroken panorama of night sky with no ground. Whales at three
depths swimming left to right across drifting thin clouds; long dotted
orbital arcs; a small moon at the far left (x 0.06, y 0.4), a crescent the
size of a lighthouse at the far right. Nothing bright enough to compete with
white text over the lower third.
